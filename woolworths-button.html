Woolworths price button

Shelf Check uses this button to collect Woolworths prices once a week.

Set it up (once)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this green button up onto the bookmarks bar:
    Collect Woolworths prices

Use it (about once a week)

  1. Go to www.woolworths.co.nz (any page).
  2. Click Collect Woolworths prices on your bookmarks bar. A green bar appears at the top of the page and shows progress.
  3. Leave the tab open for 10 to 15 minutes. When it says done, the prices are saved in your Downloads folder.
  4. That's it. Within 10 minutes your PC sends them to GitHub, shows a message in the corner of your screen, and moves the old price files to the Recycle Bin. Your website uses the new prices at its next update (6am, 12pm or 6pm).

If clicking the button does nothing on the Woolworths site, use the old way instead: open DevTools > Sources > Snippets and run collect-woolworths.js. Your PC still uploads the file for you.
